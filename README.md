# LIFE OS AI

> An AI-powered personal operating system designed to help people manage goals, tasks, habits, learning, and personal growth through personalized AI guidance.

## 🚀 Overview

LIFE OS AI is a product concept focused on solving a common problem: people use multiple disconnected tools to manage different parts of their lives, but lack a unified system that understands their goals, priorities, habits, and progress.

The product brings these areas together into one intelligent personal operating system that can provide contextual guidance and help users turn long-term goals into meaningful daily action.

## 🎯 Problem

People often manage their lives across separate tools for:

- Goals
- Tasks and to-do lists
- Habits
- Learning
- Personal development
- Planning and reflection

These tools typically operate independently, creating fragmented information and requiring users to manually connect the dots.

**The opportunity:** Create one intelligent system that understands the user's broader context and helps them make better decisions and take consistent action.

## 💡 Product Vision

> **Create an intelligent personal operating system that helps people understand where they want to go, organize what matters, and continuously improve how they get there.**

## 👤 Target Users

LIFE OS AI is designed primarily for people who:

- Have multiple personal or professional goals
- Struggle with consistency and prioritization
- Use multiple productivity or self-improvement tools
- Want personalized guidance rather than static tracking
- Want to connect daily actions with long-term outcomes

## 🧠 Jobs To Be Done

When I have multiple goals and responsibilities competing for my attention, I want an intelligent system to help me understand what matters most and what I should do next, so that I can make consistent progress without constantly managing everything manually.

## 🧩 Product Strategy

The product strategy focuses on creating a unified experience across:

**Goals → Planning → Tasks → Habits → Learning → Progress → AI Guidance**

The core product principle is to move beyond simple tracking toward **context-aware guidance and decision support**.

## 📦 MVP

The MVP focuses on the core experience required to validate the product concept.

### Core capabilities

- Goal management
- Task management
- Habit tracking
- Learning management
- Progress tracking
- Personalized AI guidance
- Unified personal dashboard

Features are prioritized using **MoSCoW prioritization** and evaluated against user value, product impact, feasibility, and MVP objectives.
